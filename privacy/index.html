Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 August 2026

Appray is a local-first product-decision tool. Everything you type stays on your device. There is no account, no backend, and no AI service involved.

No accountNothing to sign up for.
No AI APIAnswers never leave for an AI.
No trackingNot across apps or sites.
On deviceStored locally, not on a server.

Data stored on device

The app stores your project idea, selected answers, custom answers, feature scores, and generated report locally on your device. This data is used only to run the app — saving your progress and generating your report. It is not transmitted anywhere.

No account

Appray does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email, or any sign-in.

No AI service

Your answers are never sent to an AI service. Reports are generated on your device from your own choices, transparent scoring rules, and local templates.

No backend storage

In this version the app does not upload your project data to any server. There is no cloud sync.

No tracking or analytics

The app does not track you across other apps or websites, and includes no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Purchases

Appray is currently free and contains no in-app purchases. If paid features are added in a future version, the transaction will be handled entirely by Apple through In-App Purchase — Appray never sees your payment details, and Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Who is responsible

The data controller for anything this policy covers is Mariia Tararova, Primorska 8, 51000 Rijeka, Croatia — reachable at support@appray.app. Under the EU Digital Services Act this contact information also appears on the App Store product page.

The App Store transaction

Downloading the app is a transaction with Apple: Apple processes your Apple Account, device and download data as an independent controller under Apple’s privacy policy. That processing happens before Appray ever runs, and none of it reaches us.

Children

Appray is a productivity tool for founders and makers. It collects no personal data and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
